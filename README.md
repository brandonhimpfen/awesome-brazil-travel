## Official & Planning

Essential official portals, entry requirements, e-Visa information, and climate monitoring for Brazil.

- [Visit Brazil (Embratur)](https://visitbrasil.com/en/) - Official tourism portal for Brazil offering destination advice, cultural itineraries, and practical planning tips.
- [e-Visa Brazil (VFS Global Portal)](https://brazil.vfsevisa.com/) - Official electronic visa application portal for foreign travelers requiring e-Visas.
- [Ministry of Tourism (Ministério do Turismo)](https://www.gov.br/turismo/pt-br) - Official federal ministry portal providing tourism regulations and national travel sector statistics.
- [ICMBio - Chico Mendes Institute](https://www.gov.br/icmbio/pt-br) - Federal agency overseeing conservation units and national parks, including entry information and trail advisories.
- [INMET Weather (Instituto Nacional de Meteorologia)](https://portal.inmet.gov.br/) - Official national meteorological agency monitoring forecasts and regional weather alerts across Brazil.

## Transportation

Navigating intercity buses, domestic flights, and urban transit systems throughout Brazil.

- [ClickBus](https://www.clickbus.com.br/) - Major online ticket platform for booking long-distance intercity buses across Brazil.
- [BuscaÔnibus](https://www.buscaonibus.com.br/) - Search engine to compare routes, schedules, and bus companies serving national routes.
- [LATAM Airlines Brazil](https://www.latamairlines.com/br/pt) - Flag carrier offering extensive domestic flight networks linking capital cities and regional hubs.
- [GOL Linhas Aéreas](https://www.voegol.com.br/en) - Major domestic airline providing comprehensive coverage across major metropolitan areas.
- [Azul Brazilian Airlines](https://www.voeazul.com.br/br/pt/home) - Regional airline specializing in routes servicing secondary and smaller regional airports.

## Destinations & Regions

### Rio de Janeiro & Southeast

- [Visit.Rio](https://visit.rio/) - Official tourism guide for Rio de Janeiro, detailing beach safety, neighborhood cultural sights, and event schedules.
- [MetrôRio](https://www.metrorio.com.br/) - Official metro operator website for route maps, ticketing details, and operating hours in Rio de Janeiro.

### São Paulo

- [Visite São Paulo](https://visitesaopaulo.com/) - Comprehensive tourist guide for exploring São Paulo's gastronomy, art museums, and neighborhood circuits.
- [Metro SP](https://www.metro.sp.gov.br/) - Official subway network map and service updates for São Paulo.

### Northeast & Bahian Coast

- [Salvador da Bahia Tourism](https://salvadordabahia.com/) - Visitor resource highlighting the Pelourinho, Afro-Brazilian heritage routes, and coastal beaches.
- [Turismo Pernambuco](https://www.turismo.pe.gov.br/) - Travel guide covering Recife, historic Olinda, and the Fernando de Noronha archipelago.

### Amazon & North

- [Visit Amazonas](https://www.amazonas.am.gov.br/) - Official provincial portal for ecotourism, riverboat trips, and Manaus city itineraries.

### South & Pantanal

- [Viaje Paraná](https://www.viajeparana.com/) - Regional travel portal covering Curitiba, regional eco-tours, and the Iguaçu National Park.
- [Iguaçu National Park Official Guide](https://cataratasdoiguacu.com.br/) - Visitor portal for exploring the Brazilian side of the Iguaçu Falls, trail bookings, and park operating hours.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers and remote professionals taking advantage of Brazil's digital nomad options.

- [Digital Nomad Visa (Ministério da Justiça)](https://www.gov.br/mre/pt-br/assuntos/portal-consular/vistos/visto-temporario-vitem/vitem-xiv-nomade-digital) - Official Ministry of Foreign Affairs requirements for obtaining the Temporary Visa XIV for remote workers.
- [Pipa Digital Nomad Village](https://nomadpipa.com/) - Community initiative located in Pipa, Rio Grande do Norte, providing housing and networking events for remote workers.

## Safety & Cultural Etiquette

Practical advice on health precautions, local safety protocols, and cultural context.

- [ANVISA Health Requirements](https://www.gov.br/anvisa/pt-br) - National Health Surveillance Agency outlining vaccination recommendations (such as Yellow Fever) for entry.
- [Disque 190 (Emergency Services)](https://www.gov.br/pt-br) - Direct helpline number for the Military Police (Polícia Militar) for urgent safety assistance across all Brazilian states.
